Our Story

A Multi-Speciality Clinic Built Around Patients

Shifa Dental Care was set up in Phulwari Sharif, Patna, to offer a wide range of dental treatments in one place — from routine checkups to more specialised procedures — without patients needing to travel across the city for different kinds of care.

The clinic's approach centres on patient comfort: gentle handling, clear explanations before any treatment, and modern techniques delivered in a clean, well-maintained clinical environment. Whether it's a child's first checkup or a more involved procedure, the goal is the same — care that feels calm and trustworthy.
